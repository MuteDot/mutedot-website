Questions

Does it work with Zoom, Teams, Meet and Discord?

Yes. MuteDot mutes the microphone itself, so every app hears silence, including ones that aren't on that list.

What if my call app says I'm unmuted?

The app's own mute button doesn't know about MuteDot, so it can say you're unmuted while MuteDot has the microphone muted. Nothing gets through. Go by the MuteDot indicator.

Does it need internet or an account?

No. There's no account, no license key and no telemetry. It works completely offline.

How many computers can I use it on?

All of your own. Buy once, download, and install it on every computer you use.

Does it work on Linux and Wayland?

Yes, on both X11 and Wayland. Wayland doesn't let apps grab global keys, so you bind mutedot --toggle to a key in your desktop's settings. Push-to-talk isn't available on Wayland. The Linux guide has the steps.
