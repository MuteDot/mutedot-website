Privacy

Last updated: October 2026

Short version: MuteDot doesn't collect anything about you, and neither does this website.

The app

MuteDot has no account, no analytics and no telemetry, and it never connects to the internet. Your settings, such as your hotkey and where you put the indicator, are stored only on your own computer.

This website

This site uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking scripts. Like any web server, our host may keep standard request logs (IP address, browser type, pages requested) for security and to keep the site running.

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser sends Google a request, including your IP address, when a page loads.

Buying MuteDot

Checkout is handled by our payment provider, which acts as the merchant of record. It processes your name, email address and payment details under its own privacy policy. We never see your card details.

We receive your email address and order details so we can send your download, answer support requests and process refunds. We don't sell or share them, and we don't send marketing email.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, at any time. Email mail@armd.one.
